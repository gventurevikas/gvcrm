STEP 0
Journey five is the morning ritual for remote teams: homepage, conversational reporting, and leaderboards.

STEP 1
The producer signs in through Access. Session, roles, and entitled modules come from identity — not from each CRM screen reinventing security.

STEP 2
Homepage loads with what matters today: open leads, renewals due, tasks, alerts, and a leaderboard widget.

STEP 3
Daily, weekly, and monthly boards are published for the agency. Ranks show aggregates — not peer deal details the viewer should not see.

STEP 4
From any screen, they open the central ChatGPT-mini assistant — always available, always running as themselves.

STEP 5
They ask, naturally: show premium bound by producer this month as a bar chart. If details are missing, the assistant asks follow-ups instead of guessing.

STEP 6
Dashboards and Reports executes the structured spec under the user’s security model, and every run is audited in analytics.

STEP 7
The chart appears. They can drill in, export, or save the report for the team.

STEP 8
Insurance KPIs stay aligned across reporting and gamification, so the morning view matches how the agency actually sells.

STEP 9
Journey five complete: see the day, ask for insight, act with confidence.
