STEP 0
Journey six shows how teams unstick a deal together — rotting signals, feeds, mentions, and secure document sharing.

STEP 1
A deal has gone quiet. Rotting criteria flag it: too many days in stage, or no recent activity.

STEP 2
On the Kanban board, that card stands out so the problem is visible without opening a spreadsheet.

STEP 3
The rep posts on the opportunity feed — context for anyone who already has access to the record.

STEP 4
They mention their manager. The notification carries a safe snippet — restricted fields stay hidden — and a deep link back to the deal.

STEP 5
The manager opens the notification center and jumps straight into the conversation.

STEP 6
Meanwhile, the rep shares a proposal with the customer using an encrypted link — expiry, optional password, and revoke anytime. No CRM session for the external viewer.

STEP 7
Everything lands on the activity timeline. Followers stay in sync as the deal moves again.

STEP 8
Journey six complete: visibility, collaboration, and secure customer sharing in one flow.
