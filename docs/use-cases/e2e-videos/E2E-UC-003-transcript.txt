STEP 0
Journey three is about protecting the book: renewals, rotting alerts, automated outreach, and leaderboard impact.

STEP 1
A policy is approaching term. GVCRM automatically opens a renewal opportunity on the renewal pipeline and links it back to the household and policy record.

STEP 2
The account manager reviews premium, term dates, and carrier context on that renewal opportunity.

STEP 3
If nobody works it, rotting rules highlight the deal. Managers get a clear signal before the renewal slips away.

STEP 4
Workflow automation kicks in — time-based actions can prepare tasks or emails so renewals do not depend on memory alone.

STEP 5
A branded renewal template goes out, still consent-checked through Communication, so TCPA and unsubscribe rules are honored.

STEP 6
When the renewal binds, premium and retention metrics flow into the monthly leaderboard for the agency.

STEP 7
Insurance KPIs — renewals retained, premium bound, bind ratio — stay first-class for coaching and remote motivation.

STEP 8
Journey three complete: the book of business stays warm, visible, and competitive.
