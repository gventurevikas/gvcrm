STEP 0
Journey eight covers the platform economy: install a free Marketplace app safely — sandbox first, then production.

STEP 1
An admin opens the in-product Marketplace and browses trusted listings — connectors, industry packs, and extensions.

STEP 2
On the listing page they review screenshots, scopes, and publisher details before committing.

STEP 3
Install begins in sandbox. Production data and passwords stay untouched while the team evaluates the app.

STEP 4
Scopes are consented explicitly. The admin configures the app, tests critical flows, and confirms the package signature is valid.

STEP 5
When ready, they promote to production with a clear consent step. Entitlements update so the right modules appear for users.

STEP 6
Installed apps use scoped OAuth and rate limits. Operators keep a kill switch if something ever looks wrong.
