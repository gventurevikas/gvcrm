STEP 0
Journey seven is ask-and-act: the assistant runs business operations as the signed-in user — never as a superuser.

STEP 1
Central chat opens with optional record context chips, so the assistant already knows where the producer is working.

STEP 2
The producer says: add a homeowners cross-sell on the Smith household. The assistant previews the exact fields and waits for an explicit confirm.

STEP 3
After confirm, the opportunity is created under that producer’s ownership and permissions. If they could not create it in the UI, chat cannot either.

STEP 4
Next request: email them the quote. Risky actions need a clearer confirmation — send is not silent.

STEP 5
Communication prepares the message through the normal email path, still attributed to the same user.

STEP 6
Consent and do-not-contact rules still apply. The assistant cannot bypass TCPA any more than a human screen can.

STEP 7
Finally, audit and explainability: the user can ask what changed, and admins can review every assistant-initiated write.

STEP 8
Journey seven complete: helpful, powerful, and safely bounded by the user’s own access.
