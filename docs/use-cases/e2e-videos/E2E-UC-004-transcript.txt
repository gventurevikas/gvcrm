STEP 0
Journey four covers commercial guardrails: what happens when a quote discount needs approval.

STEP 1
A sales rep is finishing a quote and applies a discount above the agency threshold. The commercial intent is clear — but policy says this needs a second set of eyes.

STEP 2
Save is blocked. Validation and approval rules fire together, so the quote cannot skip governance on the UI or the API.

STEP 3
The designated approver — manager or finance — gets a real-time notification with a deep link to the request.

STEP 4
In Approvals, they review the discount, amount, and customer context, then approve or reject with comments. Multi-step chains are supported when needed.

STEP 5
On approval, the quote unlocks for send. The audit trail remains — who asked, who approved, and when.

STEP 6
Journey four complete: discounts move fast, but never without control.
